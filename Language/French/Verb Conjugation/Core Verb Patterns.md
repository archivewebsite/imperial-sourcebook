# Core Verb Patterns

This note gives a compact map of French verbs. It is for pattern recognition, not exhaustive tense study.

## Essential Verbs

| Verb | Core Meaning | Useful Forms | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `être` | to be | `je suis`, `tu es`, `il est`, `nous sommes`, `vous êtes`, `ils sont` | Sangat penting untuk identity, location, and some compound forms. |
| `avoir` | to have | `j'ai`, `tu as`, `il a`, `nous avons`, `vous avez`, `ils ont` | Also the main auxiliary for many compound forms. |
| `aller` | to go | `je vais`, `tu vas`, `il va`, `nous allons`, `vous allez`, `ils vont` | Also used for near future: `je vais étudier`. |
| `faire` | to do; to make | `je fais`, `tu fais`, `il fait`, `nous faisons`, `vous faites`, `ils font` | Appears in weather and many expressions. |

## Pattern Groups

| Pattern | Common Verbs | What To Notice | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular `-er` | `parler`, `aimer`, `travailler`, `regarder`, `écouter` | Largest regular group; many singular forms sound alike. | `Je parle français.` |
| Regular `-ir` | `finir`, `choisir`, `réussir`, `remplir` | `nous` and `vous` forms include `-iss-`. | `Nous finissons le travail.` |
| Regular `-re` | `vendre`, `attendre`, `répondre`, `perdre` | Smaller group; final consonants often appear in plural forms. | `Tu réponds vite.` |
| Modal-like verbs | `pouvoir`, `vouloir`, `devoir`, `savoir` | Often followed by another verb in infinitive. | `Je peux venir.` |
| Movement verbs | `aller`, `venir`, `arriver`, `partir`, `sortir`, `entrer` | Many use `être` as auxiliary when intransitive. | `Je suis arrivé.` |
| Reflexive verbs | `se lever`, `s'appeler`, `se souvenir` | Keep the reflexive pronoun with the subject. | `Je m'appelle Ari.` |
| Spelling-change `-er` | `manger`, `commencer`, `changer`, `nager` | Spelling adjusts to protect pronunciation. | `Nous mangeons.` |

## Auxiliary Choice

| Choice | Use | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `avoir` | default auxiliary for most verbs | `J'ai mangé.` | Ini pilihan paling umum. |
| `être` | pronominal verbs and many intransitive movement verbs | `Je suis allé.`, `Je me suis levé.` | Jangan tulis `j'ai allé`. |
| Agreement with `être` | past participle agrees with subject in many `être` constructions | `Elle est arrivée.` | Perhatikan akhir feminine or plural in writing. |
