# Phrasebook - Idioms and Collocations

Use this as a recognition-first phrase list. Learn the whole phrase, not only the main word.

## Everyday Phrases

| Phrase | Indonesian Meaning | Context | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How's it going? | Apa kabar? | casual | Common greeting among people who already have light familiarity. |
| Nice to meet you. | Senang bertemu dengan Anda. | neutral | Use when meeting someone for the first time. |
| Could you help me? | Bisakah Anda membantu saya? | polite | Softer than `Can you help me?` |
| Would you mind repeating that? | Boleh diulangi? | polite | Useful when you did not hear clearly. |
| I don't quite understand. | Saya belum sepenuhnya paham. | polite | Softer than `I don't understand.` |
| That makes sense. | Itu masuk akal. | neutral | Shows understanding. |
| I see what you mean. | Saya paham maksudmu. | neutral | Good for discussion. |
| It depends. | Tergantung. | neutral | Useful when answer changes by context. |
| I'm running late. | Saya terlambat. | neutral | Use before arriving late. |
| No worries. | Tidak apa-apa. | casual | Friendly response to thanks or apology. |
| Take your time. | Santai saja; tidak perlu buru-buru. | kind | Reassures the other person. |
| It's up to you. | Terserah kamu. | neutral | Gives choice to someone else. |
| Sounds good. | Kedengarannya bagus. | casual | Accepts a plan. |
| By the way | Ngomong-ngomong | neutral | Changes topic lightly. |
| At the moment | Saat ini | neutral | More natural than always saying `now`. |

## Collocations

| Phrase | Indonesian Meaning | Context | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| make a decision | membuat keputusan | work, study | Not `do a decision`. |
| take a break | istirahat sebentar | daily | Fixed verb-noun pair. |
| do homework | mengerjakan PR | study | Not `make homework`. |
| pay attention | memperhatikan | class, work | Often followed by `to`. |
| get in touch | menghubungi | social, work | `I'll get in touch later.` |
| keep in mind | ingatlah | advice | Means remember while deciding. |
| make progress | membuat kemajuan | study | Common learning phrase. |
| take notes | mencatat | study | `write notes` is possible, but `take notes` is common. |
| have a look | melihat sebentar | casual | Softer than `look at this`. |
| get used to | terbiasa dengan | learning | Follow with noun or `-ing`. |
| look forward to | menantikan | polite, email | Follow with noun or `-ing`. |
| keep an eye on | mengawasi | daily | Idiomatic but common. |
| catch up with | mengejar ketertinggalan; bertemu lagi | social, study | Meaning depends on context. |
| figure out | mencari tahu; memahami | casual | Useful for problem solving. |
| deal with | menangani | work, daily | Often means manage a problem. |

## Idioms

| Idiom | Indonesian Meaning | Context | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a piece of cake | sangat mudah | casual | Informal. |
| break the ice | mencairkan suasana | social | Useful for first meetings. |
| once in a while | sesekali | neutral | Frequency phrase. |
| on the other hand | di sisi lain | writing, speaking | Shows contrast. |
| in the long run | dalam jangka panjang | discussion | Good for planning. |
| out of the blue | tiba-tiba | storytelling | Something unexpected. |
| on purpose | sengaja | neutral | Opposite of `by accident`. |
| under the weather | kurang sehat | casual | Common mild illness idiom. |
| call it a day | selesai untuk hari ini | work, study | End work for now. |
| get the hang of it | mulai terbiasa | learning | Means skill starts to feel easier. |
