# Core Verb Patterns

This note gives a compact map of German verb patterns. It is for everyday recognition and production.

## Essential Verbs

| Verb | Core Meaning | Useful Forms | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sein` | to be | `ich bin`, `du bist`, `er ist`, `wir sind`, `ihr seid`, `sie/Sie sind` | Identity, state, and many essential phrases. |
| `haben` | to have | `ich habe`, `du hast`, `er hat`, `wir haben`, `ihr habt`, `sie/Sie haben` | Possession and many compound forms. |
| `werden` | to become; auxiliary | `ich werde`, `du wirst`, `er wird`, `wir werden`, `ihr werdet`, `sie/Sie werden` | Used for becoming and future-like expressions. |

## Pattern Groups

| Pattern | Common Verbs | What To Notice | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weak verbs | `machen`, `lernen`, `wohnen`, `kaufen`, `spielen` | Most regular and predictable. | `Ich lerne Deutsch.` |
| Strong verbs | `gehen`, `sehen`, `sprechen`, `fahren`, `essen` | May change stem vowel in important forms. | `Du sprichst Deutsch.` |
| Mixed verbs | `bringen`, `denken`, `kennen`, `wissen` | Regular-looking endings with internal changes. | `Ich denke daran.` |
| Modal verbs | `können`, `müssen`, `wollen`, `sollen`, `dürfen`, `mögen` | Often pair with an infinitive. | `Ich kann kommen.` |
| Separable-prefix verbs | `aufstehen`, `einkaufen`, `anrufen`, `mitbringen` | Prefix can move to the end in main clauses. | `Ich stehe früh auf.` |
| Inseparable-prefix verbs | `besuchen`, `bezahlen`, `verstehen`, `bekommen` | Prefix stays attached. | `Ich verstehe das.` |
| `-ieren` verbs | `studieren`, `telefonieren`, `organisieren` | Usually regular and international-looking. | `Sie studiert Medizin.` |

## Pattern Reminders

| Reminder | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In a simple main clause, a separable prefix can appear at the end. | `Ich rufe dich an.` | Store the dictionary form as `anrufen`. |
| With a modal verb, the second verb is usually an infinitive. | `Wir müssen lernen.` | Main action appears late. |
| Some `du` and `er/sie/es` forms change vowels. | `du sprichst`, `er fährt` | Treat these as high-priority memory items. |
| `Sie` formal uses plural-looking verb forms. | `Sprechen Sie Deutsch?` | Capital `Sie` means formal you. |
