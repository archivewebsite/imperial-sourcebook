There are good days and bad days. Today is one of the bad days.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/0*9gfF7Ys-Bew1bfy6.jpg)

> **Free** [**Access**](https://medium.com/@janelle.ngcdo/fe9f73f426fd?sk=f0aa8cfbe71349ec9086bda74a287bae)

There’s always a telling sign —  
you wake up and realise that you don’t actually want to wake up;  
==you get out of bed, and the air feels heavy, thick, as if you’re under water;  
your heart is tired and weak, your mind refuses to go to work.==

> ah, one of those days.

Sometimes, I try to bargain with it. *“Small wins build momentum”* — that’s what they all say on the podcasts.

So I try.  
Make the bed. Get ready. Drink coffee. Journal. Go for a quick run.  
Done, done, done, done, done.  
Feeling better?  
*No.*

==Because there are days you can’t really escape or avoid it, no matter how hard you try to compensate.== It hums hauntingly in your head, claims its rightful residency inside your body.

Some days, I have to accept defeat.

> Why am I like this?  
> ==Why can’t I just be== ==***okay***====?==

==But I guess being okay is not a default setting; it’s just a feeling that visits whenever it feels like it. And on days like this, even breathing alone can be counted as a success.==

We keep striving to be the best version of ourselves all the time. We expect every day to be full of sunshine and rainbows, but we cannot stop days that are gloomy and rainy — we just have to accept them.

Sit with the bad weather and let the day be.

==**Do what you can, and it should be enough.**==

==I think what scares me most about these days is how convincing they are. They make it feel like this is who I am at my core — the fog, the heaviness, the tired heart. As if the brighter parts of me were a temporary glitch, and this is the real version underneath, the one I have to live with for the rest of my life.==

==But I’ve lived long enough to know that no feeling stays forever, everything is fleeting, and everything returns.== And how marvellous is that? The realisation that we will never be just one thing, but always changing, evolving and becoming— always moving, even on days we’re stuck.

==I’ve survived every one of these days so far.==  
And I’ll get through this one too.  
And the next.  
And all the ones after that.

We’re all gonna be okay.  
We’re all gonna be not okay.  
==Nothing stays.  
Nothing lasts.==

==This too shall pass. And that should be enough to look forward to.==
