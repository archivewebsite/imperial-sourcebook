It happened in a gym yoga class

She was a beautiful young woman. Most of the guys who worked out at the Gold’s Gym on Sansom Street in Philadelphia (circa 2000) were half in lust with her.

She seemed to be one of those rare women who had everything going for her. Although she was respected she lacked any of those endearing vulnerabilities giving a man a reason to think she could benefit from relating to him.

Maybe as a psychologist she was intimidating for lots of guys.

==She kept to herself, did her exercise routine (without a trainer), showered and took off for work, wherever that may be.==

She appeared emotionally detached, unable, or more likely, unwilling to fraternize with any of the men or women who could be potential friends, or even lovers.

Although rumor had it she was single, the guys I hung with admired her from afar but were put off by her seeming unavailability.

### Yoga

Gold’s Gym was a haven for steroid-popping muscle-heads as well as many of the professional people working in the large office buildings found in Center City. The gym was open 24/7 and also attracted many of the city’s night-workers such as bartenders, policemen, firefighters, medical professionals, hospitality workers, truck drivers…..

Demand for starting a yoga class was popular enough gym management hired one of the trainers to conduct a session from 7:00–8:00 in the morning.

I had never been in a yoga class and probably couldn’t differentiate yoga from yogurt.

However our instructor (Clarance) was patient and gave the 12 of us who’d signed up for class a session of basic philosophies explaining the practice of yoga united individual conscience with the divine or universal conscience.

The class was to meet every Monday, Wednesday and Friday in a small room on the 2nd floor of the gym.

There were an equal number of men and women signed up for the gym’s initial yoga instruction, the beautiful therapist and I among them.

We lined up in two rows of six each on their mats facing each other.

The first pose the instructor usually does to beginner students is the ***table top.*** It’s exactly what the name indicates. Students knees are bent so it appears he will crawl doggie style. Arms straight with hands on the floor palms flat.

Clarance directed the men and women, mostly young professionals to keep their heads up and back straight.

“Maintain awareness of your body-mind connection and stay present with the moment, focus on your deepest desires,” Clarance explained, teaching, not demanding.

As fortune would have it, in the line-up of yoga mats on the floor of the small room Gold’s Gym allotted to this group endeavor, the unsociable counselor had placed her mat only three feet in front of mine.

Here I was: facing this beautiful elusive woman, each of us on our knees, looking straight ahead and being instructed by the newly-minted yoga master, Clarance, to, “focus on our deepest desire.”

The room was totally silent for almost fifteen minutes with 12 adults experiencing their first yoga session trying to achieve inner harmony:

A soft voice, almost inaudible, coming from the psychologist no more than three feet in front of me, ***“Since we’re already down here, Brian.”***

==I’m not quite sure that this was the internal state of a blissfully quiet mind for which we were striving.==

***But I’m all for it.***
