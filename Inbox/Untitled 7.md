**My strongest recommendations:**

**Cognitea Steep**  
This is probably my favorite. It suggests saving web content and letting it become knowledge over time. Elegant, on-brand, and not painfully literal.

**Cognitea Infuse**  
Strong if the tool injects captured web content into your notes or knowledge base. Sounds polished.

**Cognitea Webleaf**  
Nice metaphor: each saved page becomes a leaf in your knowledge garden/book/tea blend. Weirdly pleasant. Annoyingly good.

**Cognitea Brewmark**  
A blend of “brew” and “bookmark.” Very brand-specific and memorable.

**Cognitea Distill**  
Best if the clipper summarizes, extracts, cleans, or transforms webpages before saving.

**Cognitea Leaflet**  
Feels lightweight, browser-extension-friendly, and related to pages/leaves.

**Cognitea Sip**  
Short and memorable. Great if you want a small, fast capture tool.

**Cognitea Extract**  
Clear, practical, and slightly technical.

**Cognitea Gather**  
Friendly and broad. Works if the tool captures links, highlights, quotes, screenshots, notes, and metadata.

**PageSteep**  
Best standalone name if you do not want every product to start with Cognitea.
