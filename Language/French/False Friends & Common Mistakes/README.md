# False Friends & Common Mistakes

`False Friends & Common Mistakes` stores French words and patterns that often mislead learners.

## What Belongs Here

- French-English false friends with Indonesian explanations
- beginner mistakes around articles, agreement, auxiliary choice, negation, and `tu/vous`
- short corrected examples

## What Does Not

- ordinary vocabulary rows
- full grammar lessons
- rare advanced exceptions

## How To Use It

- review the misleading assumption first
- keep one clear corrected example per row
- add mistakes found in your own French writing or speaking

## Current Notes
