# False Friends and Common Mistakes

| Trap | Correct Meaning or Pattern | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aktuell` = actually | `aktuell` means current. | `Das ist aktuell wichtig.` | `Actually` is closer to `eigentlich` or `tatsächlich`. |
| `bekommen` = become | `bekommen` means get or receive. | `Ich bekomme eine E-Mail.` | `Become` is `werden`. |
| `der Chef` = chef | `der Chef` means boss. | `Mein Chef ist hier.` | Cook/chef is `der Koch` or `der Chefkoch`. |
| `das Gift` = gift | `das Gift` means poison. | `Das ist Gift.` | Gift/present is `das Geschenk`. |
| `das Handy` = handy | `das Handy` means mobile phone. | `Mein Handy ist neu.` | `handy` as adjective is `praktisch`. |
| `das Gymnasium` = gym | `das Gymnasium` is a type of secondary school. | `Sie besucht das Gymnasium.` | Sports gym is `die Turnhalle` or `das Fitnessstudio`. |
| `der Rat` = rat | `der Rat` means advice or council. | `Danke für den Rat.` | Rat animal is `die Ratte`. |
| `also` = also | German `also` often means thus or so. | `Also, wir gehen jetzt.` | English `also` is usually `auch`. |
| `bald` = bald | German `bald` means soon. | `Bis bald.` | Hairless is `kahl`. |
| `fast` = fast | German `fast` means almost. | `Ich bin fast fertig.` | Fast is `schnell`. |
| `eventuell` = eventually | `eventuell` means possibly. | `Eventuell komme ich später.` | Eventually is `schließlich` or `endlich`. |
| `sensibel` = sensible | `sensibel` means sensitive. | `Er ist sensibel.` | Sensible is `vernünftig`. |
| `sympathisch` = sympathetic | `sympathisch` means likeable or pleasant. | `Sie ist sympathisch.` | Sympathetic is `mitfühlend`. |
| `brav` = brave | `brav` means well-behaved. | `Das Kind ist brav.` | Brave is `mutig`. |
| `das Rezept` = receipt | `das Rezept` means recipe or prescription. | `Ich brauche ein Rezept.` | Receipt is `die Quittung`. |
| `die Rente` = rent | `die Rente` means pension. | `Er bekommt Rente.` | Rent is `die Miete`. |
| `der Roman` = Roman person | `der Roman` means novel. | `Ich lese einen Roman.` | Roman person is `der Römer`. |
| `die Hose` = hose | `die Hose` means trousers or pants. | `Die Hose ist schwarz.` | Hose/tube is `der Schlauch`. |
| `die Noten` = notes | `die Noten` can mean grades or musical notes. | `Meine Noten sind gut.` | Written notes are `die Notizen`. |
| `spenden` = spend | `spenden` means donate. | `Wir spenden Geld.` | Spend money is `Geld ausgeben`. |
| `übersehen` = oversee | `übersehen` often means overlook or miss. | `Ich habe den Fehler übersehen.` | Oversee is closer to `beaufsichtigen`. |
| Forgetting noun capitalization | Capitalize German nouns. | `Ich lese ein Buch.` | Lowercase nouns are a visible mistake. |
| Dropping articles too often | Keep articles with nouns. | `Das ist ein Problem.` | Articles carry gender and case. |
| Using `nicht` for all noun negation | Use `kein` for many indefinite nouns. | `Ich habe keinen Termin.` | `kein` behaves like an article. |
| Forgetting separable prefixes | Keep the prefix in the sentence. | `Ich rufe dich an.` | The prefix can move to the end. |
| Treating `Sie` and `sie` the same | Capital `Sie` means formal you. | `Sprechen Sie Deutsch?` | Lowercase `sie` can mean she or they. |
