## Embracing where you are as you grow into who you’re meant to be.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*e4Vw8MHS9RbYqU0CummaUg.jpeg)

[🍀](https://www.pinterest.com/caochinduoifox/20s-1/)

> ==The real joy hides in the dance of the journey, not the destination.==

I always wonder **what we are searching for, what we are truly looking for.** Some people say they want something that makes their life happy, some say they want peace, and some say they want money to live happily and fully.

But no one tries to understand, or at least try to see, the beauty of what they already have right now.

Maybe that’s why people always expect or blame others when things go wrong. But those who blame others are never really happy in their own lives.

[*Non-members can read here.*](https://phagunishrivastava.medium.com/0f505dcb1364?sk=d2d162ec5c1bd02117e0b96615f87ae7)

There is a strange restlessness in the human heart an insatiable pull toward more. More success, more certainty, more becoming. But in that constant reaching, **we begin to overlook the quiet beauty of what already exists within and around us.** The ordinary moments, the unnoticed joys, the soft stillness of simply being.

> The things we are searching for, or maybe looking for, are very much a result of our present attitude and intentions.

In learning to dance in the middle of the journey. In embracing the uncertainties, the detours and **even in the darkest nights, when you feel the worst version of your life, there is still something within you learning how to endure.**

Because growth is not always luminous. Sometimes, it is quiet almost invisible. Sometimes, it feels like standing still. But even then something within you is shifting.

There is a quiet grace in embracing the present nothing as something perfect but as something real. We often treat the present like a waiting room, a temporary space before life finally begins. ==**But what if this is life? And it is,**== ==not the polished version we imagine, but this raw, unfinished, beautifully imperfect moment.==

To embrace where you are is not to settle it is to acknowledge that even now, in this very phase, you are living something meaningful. There is depth in your current life, even if it feels incomplete.

**No one comes and makes your life the way you want it but you can do that for yourself.**

And the beauty of it lies in learning to dance in the middle of the journey, to embrace the hurdles even the darkest nights where you feel the worst of your life.

==***You will get there.***== ***There is no doubt about that.***

But right now this moment, this version of you, this imperfect, unfolding life it holds a beauty that is just as profound.

And this is not something to rush through but **something to gently, wholeheartedly live.**
