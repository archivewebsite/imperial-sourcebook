## For those who feel everything too deeply and carry what they can’t explain.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*4a5PYnaQWgIyfHA-4zIk4w.jpeg)

[♡](https://in.pinterest.com/sravigangula/story-ideas)

> ==Maybe you were meant to carry tenderness within you, even when the world around you expects you to be unfeeling and guarded.==

*If you’re a little like me, I think you’re scared too.* Not in ways that are obvious, not in ways people easily notice but in a quiet, constant way that lives beneath everything.

==Scared of not knowing enough, of never truly understanding anything completely.==

Scared of being uncertain, and sometimes, even scared of the fact that you feel this much at all. ==**And sometimes, it’s not even about the world outside.... it’s about the weight of your own thoughts. How deeply they go, how much they hold, how hard they are to silence.**==

Because in a world that quietly rewards perfection, there is an unspoken pressure to appear composed, certain, and untouched by doubt.

[*Non-members can read here.*](https://phagunishrivastava.medium.com/b6502f749e30?sk=0041dce7ae896d3d7f8ab3a2ffe26376)

And maybe that’s where it begins. **I have always been someone who feels… intensely.**  
I have always felt like I move through the world differently.

As if I don’t just exist in it, I absorb it. Like a sponge, I take in everything around me, often without meaning to, and always more deeply than I know how to handle. Every glance, every slight change in tone, every pause between words, it all reaches me. I don’t search for these things. **It happens without effort, as if my mind is constantly collecting fragments of everything around me.**

Every room leaves something behind in me. Every person, a trace I didn’t ask to hold. I pick up on emotions that are never spoken, on silences that stretch just a second too long, on energies that shift in ways others seem to overlook. And over time, it becomes something heavier than I can explain. Not a single weight, but a quiet accumulation….***layer upon layer of moments, feelings, impressions that don’t belong entirely to me, yet refuse to leave.***

And the most difficult part is trying to explain something that doesn’t have a clear shape.

***How do you explain that nothing is visibly wrong, and yet something inside feels unbearably full?***

That you are not overwhelmed by what happens, but by how deeply it stays?

***That you are tired, not from doing too much, but from feeling too much…. noticing too much..holding too much?***

*So you begin to adjust yourself in small, almost invisible ways.*

You soften your reactions. You silence your thoughts. You convince yourself that maybe you are overthinking, that maybe you are just too sensitive as if that is something that needs correction.

Because the world, in its own quiet way teaches you that gentleness must be managed. That depth must be controlled. That to belong, you must appear steady, unaffected, almost untouched by the chaos around you.

But in all of this, you also start to avoid the ones who are really there for you. Sometimes, you fear that if you become closer to them, you might both end up hurting each other. **So instead of being present with them, you end up pulling them away not because you don’t care, but because you care too much and believe this is the better option.**

But real truth is, what you feel is not excessive. ***It is expansive.***

==There is something deeply human, almost sacred, about being this aware.  
About noticing what others pass by. About holding emotions with such intensity, even when they were never meant for you.==

It may feel overwhelming, it may feel isolating, it may even make you retreat into yourself ( and it does) but it also means you experience life in its rawest form.

**Yes, it is heavy. Yes, it can make the world feel sharper, louder, more consuming. Yes**, there are nights when everything you’ve carried seems to surface all at once, even in the darkest nights, where you feel the worst of your life, where your thoughts feel heavier than your own breath, ***something within you is still enduring.... still holding on, even when you don’t realise it.***

But it also like, When you care, you do so with depth. When you listen, you do so with presence. When you love, you do so with a sincerity that cannot be replicated. And this is what pure beauty is.

**This type of feeling is uniquely yours.**

Even if it feels like too much sometimes, even if you wish you could quiet your mind, dim your feelings, make things lighter but there is nothing wrong with the way you are.

This world won’t always understand this kind of softness. It will ask you to be stronger in ways that feel like hardening. **But you were never meant to become hard just to belong here.**

I wish I didn’t always have this helpful reaction like so deeply to other people’s pain. It can feel overwhelming, sometimes even too much. **But I would still choose this over being distant, over not feeling at all.**

Because I know I can learn to hold it better. I can learn to steady myself.

==**It feels like both a blessing and a curse.**== ==To feel everything so deeply…==. the joy, the sorrow, the quiet ache in between.

To understand, to connect, to hold others gently. But sometimes, the same depth turns inward. And I end up hurting myself just by feeling too much.

Same days don’t stay forever and maybe all I need is to **understand myself a little more, to gently learn how to carry what I feel.**

So if you are someone who absorbs everything, who feels things you cannot always explain, who carries invisible weight behind a calm exterior, this is for you.

For the gentle parts of you that you keep trying to hide. For the softness that feels like a burden, but was never meant to be one. For the depth you’ve been taught to question, but never needed to.

==You don’t have to lose it. You just have to learn how to hold it with care, with patience, with a quiet kind of compassion.==

> Hold yourself too, the same way you hold the world, so gently even when it feels like too much.
