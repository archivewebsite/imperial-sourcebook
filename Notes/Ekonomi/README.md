# Ekonomi

`Ekonomi` stores economics, markets, business, finance, and money-related reference notes.

## What Belongs Here

- business and market notes
- finance concepts and formulas
- economics articles and source-derived money references
- spending, investing, and wealth-related notes when they are mainly analytical rather than personal advice

## What Does Not

- active financial tracking that belongs in `D:\Imperial Records\Areas\Financial Ledger`
- motivational money advice that belongs in `Motivation`
- tactical self-management advice that belongs in `Personal Advice`

## How To Use It

- keep source-specific article notes here when they support economic understanding
- move compact formulas or definitions only when another folder clearly fits better
- split child folders only after repeated topics make retrieval harder

## Review Rhythm

Review when market, business, and personal-finance notes start repeating around a stronger topic cluster.
