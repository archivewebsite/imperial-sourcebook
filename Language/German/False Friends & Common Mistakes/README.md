# False Friends & Common Mistakes

`False Friends & Common Mistakes` stores German words and patterns that often mislead English or Indonesian-speaking learners.

## What Belongs Here

- German-English false friends
- mistakes around articles, cases, `nicht/kein`, separable verbs, capitalization, and `Sie/du`
- short corrected examples

## What Does Not

- ordinary vocabulary rows
- full grammar lessons
- rare advanced exceptions

## How To Use It

- review the misleading word or pattern first
- keep one clear corrected example per row
- add mistakes from your own writing and speaking practice
