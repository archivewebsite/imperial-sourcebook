# Beginner Noun Gender List

Learn the article with the noun. In German, the article is not optional memory support; it carries gender and later case information.

## Gender Clues

| Clue | Often Signals | Examples | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `der` | masculine | `der Tag`, `der Tisch` | Learn as one chunk. |
| `die` | feminine or plural | `die Frage`, `die Bücher` | Singular `die` can be feminine; plural `die` is all genders. |
| `das` | neuter | `das Haus`, `das Kind` | Common with diminutives and many young-being nouns. |
| Endings `-ung`, `-heit`, `-keit`, `-tion`, `-schaft` | often feminine | `die Zeitung`, `die Freiheit` | Strong pattern for gender guessing. |
| Endings `-chen`, `-lein`, `-ment`, `-um` | often neuter | `das Mädchen`, `das Dokument`, `das Museum` | Exceptions still exist. |
| Days, months, seasons | often masculine | `der Montag`, `der April`, `der Winter` | Useful for time vocabulary. |
