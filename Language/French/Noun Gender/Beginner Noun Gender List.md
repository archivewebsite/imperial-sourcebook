# Beginner Noun Gender List

Learn the article with the noun. The article is part of the memory item.

## Gender Clues

| Clue | Often Signals | Examples | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `le` or `un` | masculine | `le livre`, `un café` | Artikel menunjukkan gender. |
| `la` or `une` | feminine | `la maison`, `une table` | Artikel menunjukkan gender. |
| `l'` | gender hidden by vowel sound | `l'école`, `l'homme` | Check dictionary or vocabulary note. |
| Endings like `-tion`, `-sion`, `-té` | often feminine | `la question`, `la liberté` | Many exceptions still exist. |
| Endings like `-age`, `-ment`, `-eau` | often masculine | `le fromage`, `le gouvernement`, `le bureau` | Learn exceptions separately. |
