# Cultural Notes

`Cultural Notes` stores practical notes about English communication culture, especially politeness, register, small talk, and classroom or work situations.

## What Belongs Here

- notes on how English is used politely in real situations
- differences between casual, classroom, work, and formal contexts
- reminders about English varieties when they affect understanding

## What Does Not

- country history or tourism notes
- grammar explanations without a cultural communication point
- vocabulary dictionary rows

## How To Use It

- use these notes to choose tone, not to stereotype speakers
- add examples from real conversations, emails, classes, and media
- keep notes practical and language-focused
