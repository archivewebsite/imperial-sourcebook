# False Friends & Common Mistakes

`False Friends & Common Mistakes` stores English words and patterns that look easy but often cause learner errors.

## What Belongs Here

- confusing word pairs
- common collocation and preposition mistakes
- notes where Indonesian-to-English transfer can create unnatural English

## What Does Not

- normal vocabulary rows
- rare style preferences
- long grammar explanations without examples

## How To Use It

- review the wrong assumption first, then the corrected example
- add notes from your own writing mistakes
- keep examples short and reusable

## Current Notes

- `Common Mistakes and False Friends.md`: starter table of high-frequency English learner traps.


## Review Rhythm

- Review when learner mistakes repeat enough to need new examples or consolidation.
- Keep contrast pairs and common errors here; route general grammar to grammar notes.
