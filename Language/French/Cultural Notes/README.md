# Cultural Notes

`Cultural Notes` stores practical French communication notes about politeness, greetings, register, and social distance.

## What Belongs Here

- language-culture notes that affect how French is used
- reminders about greetings, `tu/vous`, service situations, and conversation tone
- short examples with Indonesian explanations

## What Does Not

- broad tourism notes
- national stereotypes
- grammar lessons without a cultural communication point

## How To Use It

- use these notes to choose safer language in real situations
- add regional or context differences only when they affect wording
- keep examples practical
