# 16 Rumus Tenses + Contoh

## 1. Simple Present Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kebiasaan, fakta umum, atau kejadian yang berulang.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + V1 + s/es
- Negatif: S + do/does + not + V1
- Interogatif: Do/Does + S + V1

Contoh:

- Positif: I eat cookies every day.
- Negatif: I do not eat cookies every day.
- Interogatif: Do you eat cookies every day

Catatan:

- Gunakan `does` untuk subjek `he`, `she`, dan `it`.
- Gunakan tambahan `s/es` pada kata kerja jika subjeknya `he`, `she`, atau `it`.

Contoh:

- She eats cookies every day.
- She does not eat cookies every day.
- Does she eat cookies every day

## 2. Present Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang sedang berlangsung sekarang.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + am/is/are + V-ing
- Negatif: S + am/is/are + not + V-ing
- Interogatif: Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I am eating cookies now.
- Negatif: I am not eating cookies now.
- Interogatif: Are you eating cookies now

## 3. Present Perfect Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kejadian yang sudah selesai atau sudah terjadi, tetapi masih memiliki hubungan dengan masa sekarang.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + have/has + V3
- Negatif: S + have/has + not + V3
- Interogatif: Have/Has + S + V3

Contoh:

- Positif: I have eaten cookies.
- Negatif: I have not eaten cookies.
- Interogatif: Have you eaten cookies

Catatan:

- Gunakan `has` untuk subjek `he`, `she`, dan `it`.
- Gunakan `have` untuk subjek `I`, `you`, `we`, dan `they`.

Contoh:

- She has eaten cookies.
- She has not eaten cookies.
- Has she eaten cookies

## 4. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang sudah berlangsung sejak masa lalu dan masih berlanjut sampai sekarang, atau baru saja selesai dengan dampak yang masih terasa.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + have/has + been + V-ing
- Negatif: S + have/has + not + been + V-ing
- Interogatif: Have/Has + S + been + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I have been eating cookies for two hours.
- Negatif: I have not been eating cookies for two hours.
- Interogatif: Have you been eating cookies for two hours

## 5. Simple Past Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kejadian yang terjadi dan selesai di masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + V2
- Negatif: S + did + not + V1
- Interogatif: Did + S + V1

Contoh:

- Positif: I ate cookies yesterday.
- Negatif: I did not eat cookies yesterday.
- Interogatif: Did you eat cookies yesterday

## 6. Past Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + was/were + V-ing
- Negatif: S + was/were + not + V-ing
- Interogatif: Was/Were + S + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I was eating cookies last night.
- Negatif: I was not eating cookies last night.
- Interogatif: Were you eating cookies last night

Catatan:

- Gunakan `was` untuk subjek `I`, `he`, `she`, dan `it`.
- Gunakan `were` untuk subjek `you`, `we`, dan `they`.

## 7. Past Perfect Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kejadian yang sudah selesai sebelum kejadian lain di masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + had + V3
- Negatif: S + had + not + V3
- Interogatif: Had + S + V3

Contoh:

- Positif: I had eaten cookies before dinner.
- Negatif: I had not eaten cookies before dinner.
- Interogatif: Had you eaten cookies before dinner

## 8. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang telah berlangsung selama periode tertentu sebelum kejadian lain di masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + had + been + V-ing
- Negatif: S + had + not + been + V-ing
- Interogatif: Had + S + been + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I had been eating cookies for an hour.
- Negatif: I had not been eating cookies for an hour.
- Interogatif: Had you been eating cookies for an hour

## 9. Simple Future Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kejadian yang akan terjadi di masa depan.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + will + V1
- Negatif: S + will + not + V1
- Interogatif: Will + S + V1

Contoh:

- Positif: I will eat cookies tomorrow.
- Negatif: I will not eat cookies tomorrow.
- Interogatif: Will you eat cookies tomorrow

Catatan:

- `Shall` dapat digunakan dalam situasi tertentu, terutama dengan subjek `I` dan `we`, tetapi dalam American English modern, `will` lebih umum digunakan.

## 10. Future Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang akan sedang berlangsung pada waktu tertentu di masa depan.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + will + be + V-ing
- Negatif: S + will + not + be + V-ing
- Interogatif: Will + S + be + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I will be eating cookies tomorrow night.
- Negatif: I will not be eating cookies tomorrow night.
- Interogatif: Will you be eating cookies tomorrow night

## 11. Future Perfect Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kejadian yang akan sudah selesai sebelum waktu tertentu atau sebelum kejadian lain di masa depan.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + will + have + V3
- Negatif: S + will + not + have + V3
- Interogatif: Will + S + have + V3

Contoh:

- Positif: I will have eaten cookies by noon.
- Negatif: I will not have eaten cookies by noon.
- Interogatif: Will you have eaten cookies by noon

## 12. Future Perfect Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang akan sudah berlangsung selama periode tertentu sampai waktu tertentu di masa depan.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + will + have + been + V-ing
- Negatif: S + will + not + have + been + V-ing
- Interogatif: Will + S + have + been + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I will have been eating cookies for two hours.
- Negatif: I will not have been eating cookies for two hours.
- Interogatif: Will you have been eating cookies for two hours

## 13. Simple Past Future Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan rencana, prediksi, atau kejadian masa depan yang dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + would + V1
- Negatif: S + would + not + V1
- Interogatif: Would + S + V1

Contoh:

- Positif: I would eat cookies.
- Negatif: I would not eat cookies.
- Interogatif: Would you eat cookies

Catatan:

- Dalam banyak konteks, bentuk ini juga dikenal sebagai `future in the past` atau bentuk conditional.
- `Should` tidak selalu sama dengan `would`. Dalam American English modern, `should` lebih sering bermakna “seharusnya” atau “sebaiknya”, bukan sekadar masa depan di masa lalu.

## 14. Past Future Continuous Tense

Fungsi: Menyatakan kegiatan yang akan sedang berlangsung di masa depan, dilihat dari sudut pandang masa lalu.

Rumus:

- Positif: S + would + be + V-ing
- Negatif: S + would + not + be + V-ing
- Interogatif: Would + S + be + V-ing

Contoh:

- Positif: I would be eating cookies.
- Negatif: I would not be eating cookies.
- Interogatif: Would you be eating cookies

## 15. Past Future Perfect Tense
