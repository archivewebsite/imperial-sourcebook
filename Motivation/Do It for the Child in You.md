## The part of you that still believes in miracles.
✿

==There is a== ==*quiet part of you*== ==that still remembers who you were before the world started telling you who to be. It doesn’t speak loudly, it doesn’t interrupt your busy days, but it lingers somewhere in the background of your choices, your fears, your longings. And sometimes, without even realizing it, everything you do is just an attempt to return to that feeling you once had== ==**as a child the feeling of being enough without trying to perform.**==

> Non-members can read here

==Sometimes we think that we have to do certain things, and we get involved in so many things as well. We desire a lot, but at the end of it all, what we really want is for our childhood version to be seen. We all have that feeling.== ==The things we couldn’t feel or experience in our childhood, we try to do them in our adulthood.==

We grow up thinking we have to become something. We begin to collect expectations, not just from others but from ourselves too. We start believing that we need to fit into certain spaces, achieve certain things, prove something to someone. **And in doing that, we slowly move away from the simplest truth that before all of this, we were already whole.**

At the end of all our desires, all our chasing, what we really want is not success in the way we define it now, but recognition.

Not from this world, **but from ourselves.**

We want to feel seen in the way we once felt, without conditions.

I feel like that so many insecurities, worries, personality traits, and even love patterns, the way we respond to love, the way we protect ourselves, the way we hesitate before expressing what we feel are already built at that age.

**And when we see this whole big world doing things, we find ourselves new to it. Of course, we are but in all of this, we forget ourselves. We feel lost at times.**

But in the process of figuring it all out, **We forget that we have to be seen first by ourselves.** ==We forget that the approval we are searching for externally was always meant to come from within.== And maybe that is why even when things look right on the outside, something still feels slightly off. Because a part of us is still waiting to be acknowledged, still waiting to be chosen.

==I think about the smallest memories sometimes, the ones that didn’t feel important back then but now carry a kind of warmth that nothing else can replace. I remember going to my nani’s house, where she would bring us a whole tub of ice cream. It wasn’t just about the ice cream== ==***it was about the way we laughed, the way we didn’t think about anything else, the way happiness existed in that exact moment without needing a reason***====. And I remember how we used to say,== ==so innocently, that when we grow up, we’ll have as much of it as we want.==

And now we have grown up. We have the ability to give ourselves those small joys, to create those moments again, and yet we hesitate. ==**We delay happiness as if it is something that needs to be earned.**== **We tell ourselves “later” so many times that we forget how to live in “now.”**

> Somewhere along the way, we stopped allowing ourselves to feel joy so freely.

Maybe **healing or be happy in present isn’t as complicated as we make it seem.** ==It’s not always about fixing everything at once or becoming a completely new version of yourself. It’s simply about returning to the things that once made you feel light, to the moments that didn’t ask anything from you, to the version of you that existed without doubt.== It’s in choosing small joys without guilt, in allowing yourself to rest without questioning it, in doing things just because they make your heart feel a little softer.

*When you start doing things for the child in you.*

> Life feels less like a race and more like a space you can actually live in.

You stop trying so hard to become, and you start allowing yourself to be.

So the next time you feel lost, or unsure, or a little too caught up in everything, pause for a moment and ask yourself something simple,

==**What would make the younger version of me smile right now?**==

And then, if you can, do that.

==Not because it solves everything, but because it reminds you of something you were never supposed to forget.==

==**At the end of it all,**== ==you were never meant to abandon yourself in the process of growing up.==

==And sometimes,== ==**I pause and think about her the younger version of me, the one who only wanted to feel seen, to feel happy in the smallest things.**== ==I== ==wonder if this version of me would make her proud… and I think, yes, she would be. Not because I have everything figured out, or because I became something big, but because I am trying.==

> Trying to understand myself, trying to be gentle with my heart, trying to choose what feels right instead of what feels expected.

And maybe that’s for her. ==**Maybe all she ever wanted was to see me not give up on myself.**==

> **You were meant to carry that child within you, to protect it, to listen to it, to choose it again and again, in the smallest ways.**

==*So do it for the child in you.*==

Because in choosing the part of your little self, you might finally feel like you’ve come home to yourself.
