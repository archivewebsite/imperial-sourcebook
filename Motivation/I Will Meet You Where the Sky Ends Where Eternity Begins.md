# I Will Meet You Where the Sky Ends Where Eternity Begins

## A poem

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*mStzFmMur2gN1CM4sgrEiQ.png)

Image From Pinterest, Edited on Canva

*I am not* ***waiting for you****  
in places that have names,  
not* ***in my memories****  
that ache when touched*

***i am learning to exist****  
in the in-between —  
where endings forget to be endings  
and beginnings don’t rush to prove themselves*

==***somewhere beyond the last stretch of blue,***====*  
where the sky exhales into something endless,  
i will be there*==

==*i like to think  
*====***my atoms will recognize yours***== ==*again*==*  
like old friends in a crowded universe  
bumping into each other  
****and laughing*** *without needing a reason*

==*maybe*== ==***i’ll be the wind***==***  
that pauses just long enough****  
to brush against your cheek  
the way i used to, familiar.*

***i’ll be the silence  
that doesn’t feel empty  
but full —****  
like something sacred is about to be said*

***you won’t have to search for me  
— not really***

==*because love,  
we held without knowing how to measure,  
doesn’t disappear  
it just learns new ways to exist*==

==*it hides in light,  
in the hush between two heartbeats,  
*====*in the way your hands still remember  
what it felt like to be held*==

*and when your days feel too heavy,  
when the world forgets how to be gentle with you,  
****look up, because i’ll be your sky —***

==*that’s where everything  
loosens its grip on time  
*====***that’s where i’ll meet you***==

*not as who i was,  
not as who you remember,  
but* ***as something infinite.***

*a quiet continuation*

==*and*== ==***you —  
you’ll arrive as stardust too***====*  
unburdened by memory  
but somehow  
still carrying me  
*====***we’ll exist  
like rivers meeting the ocean***== ***—***

*and* ==*when you stand there,*==*  
on the edge of everything you know,  
feeling the pull of something vast*

**that will be me —**

==***meeting you  
where the sky ends,  
where eternity  
finally  
begins.***==
