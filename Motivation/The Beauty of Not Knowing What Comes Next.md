> only when we surrender our insistence on knowing can we truly be in awe and amazed by the immense creativity of the universe

I’m a Scorpio with Virgo rising, so that means I’m spiritually a control freak. Everything I do must follow a pristine plan with a colour-coded flowchart, every plan has sub-plans with matching escape routes, hell, I even have a time block for when to cry.

I’ve always found comfort in doing this. It felt like I was superior to the universe somehow, as if, despite how chaotic the outside world seemed to be, I was still in control of my own, and that was enough.

==Whenever I’m travelling, for example, I would investigate the outlines of the city on Google Maps — learn its street names, memorise the shapes of its lanes, know about all the popular places and destinations — so that when I actually arrive, I would have already obtained a feeling of certainty, of knowing where to go and what to do.== **It’s like I’ve bargained with time itself to grant me a glimpse of the future in exchange for my laborious devotion to predicting it.**

But things started to fall apart when I reached adulthood. The currents of life grew stronger, pulling me away from my tidy forecasts. Disappointment came in waves. Regret arrived in storms. And soon I lost control of my vessel, lost at sea with no compass, no map, nothing.

The five-year plan did not survive its first month.

The manifestation board now looks like a collection of disillusionment.

Question marks lingering in the air, suspended in time — Why? How? When?

==Nothing follows my wish anymore,== and I spend days screaming into the abyss, hoping it might echo back some direction, some reassurance that I am not drifting aimlessly. That I am, somehow, still inching my way toward whatever “right path” I once believed existed.

But nothing answers, and I was left not knowing.

==Things began to soften the moment I stopped fighting the uncertainty.== When I finally loosened my grip, I realised that maybe not knowing contains its own kind of beauty that I had failed to see. ==Life becomes strangely more interesting when you don’t know what’s coming.==

==It’s like when you watch a movie or read a book, you don’t stay because you already know the ending, you stay because you know nothing at all, because you’re willing to be surprised, because you’re curious enough to turn the page without needing to control the storyline.==

Only when we surrender our insistence on knowing can we truly be in awe and amazed by the immense creativity of the universe (or whichever God or creator you believe in).

> “Let go or be dragged”

We humans are nothing more than stardust. We arrive, we learn, we dance, we ache, we love, we break, we rebuild — over and over again — until we vanish back into whatever silence we came from. We are small beings in an ever-expanding system, desperately trying to map out a life we barely understand.

At the end of the day, we know so little about living, about loving, even about ourselves. So why do we cling so stubbornly to the illusion of certainty? Why do we treat life like a checklist instead of the wild, unpredictable experience it was always meant to be?

I’m at a point in my life where everything feels uncertain, as if one tiny decision can change the whole trajectory of my life. But I’ve come to understand that the *only* thing you need to know is that **whatever happens, you can face it with grace.** To know that whatever life throws at you, you can always come back — as you’ve done a million times before — with more knowledge, more courage, more trust.

==I used to think control made life== ==*safer*====. But now I’m beginning to believe that uncertainty is what makes life== ==*meaningful*====.== Because if I already knew every twist and turn, every rise and fall — what miracle would there be left to witness? What story would there be left to live?

==That’s the beauty of not knowing what comes next. To stay open. To stay curious. To stay alive to the possibility that the next chapter could be far grander than anything we’ve dared to plan. And we will welcome it with open hearts.==

*i don’t know what comes next;
but i know i will meet it
with all that i am,
with all that i’ve learned,
with all that i can love.*
