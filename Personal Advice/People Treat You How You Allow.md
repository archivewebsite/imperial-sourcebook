## Every time you stay silent when something hurts you, teach them it’s ok to cross the line


You can think of your time and love as energy, and give it based on how much they deserve, while saving some for yourself.
*That is called setting boundaries*.

==You pour yourself down for people just to fit in, but in the end, you are left alone with guilt and burden==. You think trying is the ultimate solution to protect relationships, but you forget that watering plastic flowers won’t make them bloom.

People don’t deserve the amount of energy you have reserved for yourself, and you need to protect it by explaining clear rules to those who need to know them. ==Respect is not demanded—it is earned, and you earn respect by raising your standards.==

==The love you give must be equal to the love you receive because at the end of the day sun must set so flowers may rest from smiling all day.==

*How people treat you explains what they value about you*.

If they call you slurs even as a joke, then they see you like that. Besides close friendship, if you have a really good understanding, then it’s okay, but if that friend uses the same language in front of others, then that friend is toxic, and they are damaging your values and degrading your standards.

Words, tho easy on the tongue, hold the weight of a thousand swords. When you don’t control what you are being labeled as, you lose your values.

*You can’t pour from an empty cup.*

Friends should know their limits, and those limits include how they present you when you are with other people. This is called a boundary for friendship, and that should be outspoken, no matter how close they are.

==Every relationship, whether with friends, siblings, or parents, should be within its boundaries. You should have clear boundaries, and they should be placed in their respective place.==

You are not building a wall for isolation. You are establishing rules that will safeguard your social status and mental well-being. ==When you set boundaries, you build self-respect, and you protect your image and energy,== which is not an act of selfishness.

==In the end, people always treat you the way they believe you deserve, so standing up for yourself is a step to tell people what you're worth and that you have standards and you have self-respect, and they must know their limits.==
