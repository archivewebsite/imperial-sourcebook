# Cultural Notes

These notes focus on practical communication choices in French.

| Topic | Practical Note | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start with `Bonjour` | In shops and service situations, greet before asking. | `Bonjour, je voudrais...` | Langsung meminta bisa terdengar kasar. |
| End politely | Short interactions often still need a closing. | `Merci, au revoir.` | Jangan hanya diam lalu pergi. |
| `tu` vs `vous` | Use `vous` with strangers, formal contexts, and groups. | `Vous pouvez répéter ?` | `tu` menandakan kedekatan. |
| Asking to switch to `tu` | Ask before becoming familiar. | `On peut se tutoyer ?` | Artinya boleh pakai `tu`? |
| `s'il vous plaît` | Use it with requests. | `Un café, s'il vous plaît.` | Sama pentingnya dengan `please`. |
| `je voudrais` | Softer for ordering or requesting. | `Je voudrais une baguette.` | Lebih sopan daripada `je veux`. |
| La bise | Cheek-kiss greetings depend on relationship, region, age, and setting. | Wait and observe. | If unsure, a handshake or polite distance is safer. |
| Handshake | Common in more formal introductions. | `Bonjour, enchanté.` | Neutral and safe. |
| Informal `ça va ?` | Good with familiar people. | `Salut, ça va ?` | Avoid as first line in very formal settings. |
| Food phrases | `Bon appétit` is a normal polite phrase before eating. | `Bon appétit !` | Use before a meal. |
| Sensitive questions | Soften personal questions. | `Si ce n'est pas indiscret...` | Useful before asking something private. |
| Register | Written French often keeps fuller forms than casual speech. | `Je ne sais pas.` vs `Je sais pas.` | Learn both; write the full form first. |
