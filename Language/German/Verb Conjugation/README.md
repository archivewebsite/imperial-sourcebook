# Verb Conjugation

`Verb Conjugation` stores German verb pattern notes for practical reading and sentence building.

## What Belongs Here

- high-frequency verbs grouped by weak, strong, mixed, modal, auxiliary, separable, and inseparable patterns
- short form reminders for the verbs most likely to appear early
- examples with Indonesian meanings

## What Does Not

- full tense-table collections
- noun gender lists
- general grammar notes unrelated to verbs

## How To Use It

- learn `sein`, `haben`, and `werden` first
- group verbs by pattern before memorizing isolated forms
- mark separable-prefix verbs clearly
