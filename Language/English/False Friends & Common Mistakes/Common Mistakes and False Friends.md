# Common Mistakes and False Friends

Use this table to catch errors before they become habits.

| Wrong Assumption | Correct Use | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `information` can be plural. | Use `information` as uncountable. | `I need more information.` | Bukan `informations`. |
| `advice` can be plural. | Use `advice` or `pieces of advice`. | `She gave me advice.` | Bukan `advices`. |
| `discuss about` is normal. | Use `discuss` without `about`. | `We discussed the plan.` | `discuss` sudah berarti membahas tentang. |
| `listen music` is enough. | Use `listen to`. | `I listen to music.` | `listen` perlu `to`. |
| `explain me` is normal. | Use `explain something to someone`. | `Please explain this to me.` | Orangnya datang setelah `to`. |
| `say me` is normal. | Use `tell me`. | `Tell me the truth.` | `say` untuk kata-katanya; `tell` untuk orang. |
| `borrow` and `lend` are the same. | `borrow` receives; `lend` gives temporarily. | `Can I borrow your pen?` | `lend me` berarti meminjamkan ke saya. |
| `fun` and `funny` are the same. | `fun` enjoyable; `funny` humorous or strange. | `The game was fun.` | `funny` bisa berarti lucu atau aneh. |
| `price` and `prize` are the same. | `price` cost; `prize` award. | `The price is high.` | `prize` adalah hadiah/lomba. |
| `remember me` means `remind me`. | Use `remind` when someone helps you remember. | `Please remind me tomorrow.` | `remember` dilakukan oleh diri sendiri. |
| `economic` and `economical` are the same. | `economic` about economy; `economical` saves money. | `This car is economical.` | `economic policy`, tapi `economical car`. |
| `sensible` means sensitive. | `sensible` means reasonable; `sensitive` means easily affected. | `That is a sensible choice.` | Ini false friend untuk banyak pelajar. |
| `actual` means current. | `actual` means real; `current` means sekarang. | `The actual cost was higher.` | `actual` bukan `aktual` dalam bahasa Indonesia. |
| `eventually` means possibly. | `eventually` means finally after time. | `He eventually agreed.` | Bukan `mungkin`; itu `possibly`. |
| `make homework` is natural. | Use `do homework`. | `I do homework at night.` | Collocation tetap. |
| `do a mistake` is natural. | Use `make a mistake`. | `I made a mistake.` | Collocation tetap. |
| `depends of` is natural. | Use `depends on`. | `It depends on the weather.` | Preposisi tetap. |
| `responsible of` is natural. | Use `responsible for`. | `She is responsible for the report.` | Hafalkan sebagai chunk. |
| `married with` is natural. | Use `married to`. | `He is married to Ana.` | Preposisi berbeda dari terjemahan langsung. |
| `different with` is natural. | Use `different from` in standard English. | `This is different from mine.` | `different than/to` exists in some varieties, but `from` is safest. |
| `I am agree` is natural. | Use `I agree`. | `I agree with you.` | `agree` is a verb, not an adjective here. |
| `I have 17 years` is natural. | Use `I am 17 years old`. | `I am 17.` | English uses `be` for age. |
| `very better` is natural. | Use `much better`. | `This is much better.` | Comparatives use `much`, not `very`. |
| `more easy` is always correct. | Use `easier` for common short adjectives. | `This is easier.` | Many one-syllable adjectives take `-er`. |
| `people is` is natural. | Use `people are`. | `People are waiting.` | `people` is plural in standard use. |
