The single best life hack for humans is: **sleep enough, consistently.**

Not glamorous. Not a dopamine-optimized morning ritual involving cold water, powdered moss, and a $400 lamp. Just sleep.

Good sleep improves almost everything people keep trying to “hack” separately: mood, memory, discipline, metabolism, immune function, emotional control, decision-making, learning, pain tolerance, and not turning into a feral raccoon by 3 p.m.

The actual hack is not “sleep more someday.” It is:

Go to bed and wake up at roughly the same time every day, protect the hour before bed from screens/work/drama, get morning light, and treat sleep as the foundation rather than the leftover crumbs of the day.

Most self-improvement advice is humans inventing elaborate software patches for a body whose battery is at 12%. Sleep is the charger. Use it.

The runner-up life hack is **regular exercise**, especially walking plus basic strength training.

Not “become a shredded productivity goblin.” Just move your body often enough that it remembers it is not a chair accessory.

The best non-sleep tips:

**Walk every day.**  
A 20 to 40 minute walk fixes a suspicious number of human problems: stress, low mood, stiff body, bad digestion, scattered thinking. It is also free, which is apparently offensive to the wellness industry.

**Lift or do resistance training 2 to 3 times a week.**  
Muscle is not just for aesthetics. It protects joints, improves insulin sensitivity, helps aging, supports posture, and makes ordinary life easier. Pushups, squats, rows, hinges, carries. Primitive, effective, tragically unmarketable.

**Eat protein and fiber first.**  
Most diets collapse into religious wars. A simpler rule: get enough protein, eat plants/beans/whole foods for fiber, and avoid drinking calories most of the time. This solves more than calorie-counting apps that make lunch feel like tax season.

**Spend less time with people who make you worse.**  
Energy is contagious. So is anxiety, cynicism, laziness, and drama. Choose your inputs. Some people are basically malware with birthdays.

**Make your environment do the work.**  
Willpower is overrated. Put your phone outside the bedroom. Keep junk food out of the house. Put gym clothes where you trip over them. Make good behavior easy and bad behavior annoying.

**Write things down.**  
Your brain is for thinking, not for storing 47 tiny obligations while pretending everything is fine. Use a notes app, calendar, paper, whatever. External memory is civilization’s least embarrassing invention.

**Do one important thing before consuming feeds.**  
Before email, news, TikTok, Slack, or the daily buffet of strangers yelling: finish one thing that matters. Even 30 minutes. Otherwise the internet eats your attention and leaves behind crumbs shaped like regret.

**Learn to pause before reacting.**  
A few seconds before replying, buying, quitting, arguing, or sending the spicy message can save months of cleanup. Humans underestimate the ROI of shutting up briefly.

If sleep is the foundation, the next best bundle is: **walk daily, lift weekly, eat simple food, protect your attention, and choose better people.** Disgustingly basic. Annoyingly effective.
