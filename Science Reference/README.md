# Science Reference

`Science Reference` stores compact science theory references such as formulas, constants, theorems, laws, and reusable conceptual summaries.

## What Belongs Here

- formulas, constants, laws, and theorems as theory
- compact science reference notes intended for lookup
- reusable explanations that are not tied to one worked problem
- future subject folders once a domain grows enough to need them

## What Does Not

- worked solutions and practice breakdowns that belong in `Bahas Soal`
- broad source-driven article notes that still belong in `Notes`
- study plans or learning process notes that belong in `Learning & Skills`
- existing science, math, or `Bahas Soal` notes unless they are intentionally promoted later

## How To Use It

- keep entries short enough for reference use
- use one formula family, law, theorem, or constant group per note when possible
- link worked examples in `Bahas Soal` instead of copying full solution paths here
- do not bulk-move existing science material here without a dedicated review pass

## Review Rhythm

- Review when formulas or theory notes begin duplicating across `Notes`, `Learning & Skills`, and `Bahas Soal`.
- Create subject child folders only when repeated notes make retrieval better.
