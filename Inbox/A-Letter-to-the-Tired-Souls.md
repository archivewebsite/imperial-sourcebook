## A Poem

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*yr43SShqCQ9ZsilmCexy_g.png)

Image From Pinterest, Edited on Canva

***My dearest sunshines*** 💗 ***—***

*For the days you forget your light  
*==***you deserve to feel alive***==

==*I know it’s hard for you,  
and it makes me sad  
*====*that you don’t see yourself  
the way*== ==***I see you.***==

*Sometimes these are the things  
that get us out of life,  
and the way you have it  
hurts so much.  
****I miss your smile.***

==***Your eyes lit up  
the whole room,***====*  
just to hear your laugh.  
*====*I miss the way you accepted,  
the way you looked in the mirror  
without telling yourself  
how bad you look.*====***I miss the ways  
you didn’t think you’re a failure.***==

*Cause everyone else  
has made mistakes.*

==***We all have flaws***====***,***====*  
and we’re all not perfect*==*.  
It hurts to see  
that there’s no one around her.  
Looks like the pain  
in her eyes.*

*But I’m a stranger.  
I see how heavy her heart is,  
****and how comforting  
the sorrow is for her.***

==*How afraid of happiness  
is your heart,  
because it’s gone  
in the end, right?*==

==***You don’t know  
how effective you are  
in this world,***====*  
and it’s sad  
*====*that*== ==***your demons  
are fighting you,***====*  
wanting to keep you,  
because you transform yourself.*==

*It’s the simple  
and l* ***ittle things  
that light up  
someone’s entire world.****  
Maybe the little smile  
from your lips,  
the way you look  
at things you’re passionate about,  
*==***the way you make yourself happy  
even though it’s hard for you lately.***==

*The way you zoom out  
and go in your own world.*

==***You brighten up the world  
***====w== ==*hen you*== ==***stop shrinking***====*  
to fit places you’ve outgrown  
It means so much to me  
that*== ==***you are here,  
existing.***==

==*But*== ==***I don’t want you  
to just exist.***==

==***You deserve to feel alive.***====*  
You deserve to wake up early  
and feel good about yourself.  
*====***You deserve to feel something,***====*  
feel every second  
of being alive*==*.*

==***And I hope one day  
you will fall in love  
with being alive.***==

==**Authors Note:**== ==To all my cutu readers who’ve had heavy days but still show up — you are seen.== ==Even the smallest effort counts more than you think.  
====You deserve to feel alive💗====  
If you’re here, reading this, breathing through another day you’re already doing enough.== ==Keep going, at your own pace. Keep choosing yourself, even in the smallest ways.==

***— With so much softness and love,  
from one healing heart to another💗***
