# Practical Grammar Notes

These notes are for practical use. They avoid tense tables and formal syntax rules.

| Focus | Practical Point | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Articles | French usually needs an article before a noun. | `le livre`, `une table` | Jangan sering menghapus artikel seperti dalam bahasa Indonesia. |
| Gender | Learn each noun with `le` or `la`. | `la maison`, `le pain` | Artikel membantu mengingat gender. |
| Plural | Plural articles are often more important than final letters in speech. | `les livres`, `des amis` | Banyak akhir `-s` tidak terdengar. |
| Partitive articles | Use partitives for an unspecified amount. | `du pain`, `de la soupe` | Kurang lebih seperti `some`, tetapi sering wajib dalam French. |
| Negation with `de` | After many negative sentences, `un`, `une`, `des`, `du`, and `de la` become `de`. | `Je n'ai pas de stylo.` | Bukan `pas un stylo` untuk pola umum. |
| Contractions | `à` and `de` contract with `le` and `les`. | `au café`, `du marché`, `aux amis`, `des enfants` | `à la` and `de la` do not contract. |
| Agreement | Adjectives usually match noun gender and number. | `un petit café`, `une petite maison` | Bentuk kata sifat berubah mengikuti benda. |
| Negation frame | Basic negation often surrounds the conjugated verb. | `Je ne comprends pas.` | Dalam percakapan, `ne` sering hilang, tetapi tulis bentuk lengkap. |
| Questions | French questions can be casual, neutral, or formal. | `Tu viens ?`, `Est-ce que tu viens ?` | Pilih sesuai situasi. |
| `tu` and `vous` | `tu` is familiar; `vous` is formal or plural. | `Comment allez-vous ?` | Aman gunakan `vous` dengan orang baru. |
| `c'est` vs `il est` | `c'est` often introduces or identifies; `il est` often describes known things. | `C'est un professeur.` | Topik ini perlu contoh, bukan terjemahan kata per kata. |
| Common prepositions | `à`, `de`, `en`, `dans`, and `chez` are learned through phrases. | `chez moi`, `en France`, `à Paris` | Hafalkan dengan konteks. |
| Object pronouns | Pronouns often appear before the conjugated verb. | `Je le vois.` | Jangan selalu mengikuti urutan English. |
| Pronunciation and grammar | Written endings can matter even when silent. | `ils parlent` sounds close to `il parle` | Tulisan tetap menunjukkan subjek dan agreement. |
| Weather | Many weather phrases use fixed expressions. | `Il pleut.`, `Il fait froid.` | Jangan terjemahkan `it makes rain`. |
