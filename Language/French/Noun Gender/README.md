# Noun Gender

`Noun Gender` stores French noun lists with articles so gender is learned together with vocabulary.

## What Belongs Here

- French noun lists with `le`, `la`, `l'`, or plural article clues
- gender pattern reminders and important exceptions
- Indonesian translations for quick review

## What Does Not

- general vocabulary files, which belong in `../Vocabulary`
- verb conjugation notes
- long grammar theory

## How To Use It

- learn every noun with its article
- add plural forms only when they are useful or irregular
- mark exceptions that are easy to forget
