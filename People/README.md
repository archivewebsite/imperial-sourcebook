# People

`People` stores canonical reference pages for people who appear repeatedly across Sourcebook notes.

## What Belongs Here

- authors, mentors, scientists, founders, researchers, or public thinkers referenced by multiple notes
- short pages that explain why the person matters inside this vault
- links to the notes where their ideas, advice, or work appears

## What Does Not

- full biographies with time-sensitive details unless the source note already supports them
- one-off names that do not yet need a canonical home
- source clippings that still belong in `Clippings`

## How To Use It

- keep pages minimal and vault-focused
- use the person's normal public name as the filename
- link to source notes, topic MOCs, and closely related people when useful

## Review Rhythm

- Review when author wikilinks accumulate without a matching page.
- Expand a page only when the person becomes a recurring reference across the vault.
