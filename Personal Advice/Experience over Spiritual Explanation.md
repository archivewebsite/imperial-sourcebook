# Experience over Spiritual Explanation

## When words start losing their grip.

Have you seen people talk about spirituality for hours and still feel untouched by it

They quote masters, repeat ideas, explain concepts, and yet, when life hits, none of it holds them steady.

It may seem cruel that, despite their understanding, they cannot incorporate it when they need it most.

That gap is where the real journey begins.

## You’ve recognized the subtle trap.

The mind is clever. It wants to feel ahead, informed, and elevated.

But now you have realized how the mind enjoys using the right words in the right places and being seen as someone who *gets it.*

And now, when you speak what you have not lived, you notice a discomfort within.

You have seen the pattern that when you spoke from borrowed knowledge, you convinced yourself that you had arrived. It effectively buried the tiny, uncomfortable feeling of not experiencing it.

But now this self-betrayal chokes you.

Borrowed clarity has a certain shine. It earns attention and respect.

And that is where many seekers get stuck.

Not because they lack sincerity. But because the ego finds a new identity in spirituality itself.

They may not even notice when sharing shifts from exploration to subtle proving. From learning to displaying.

There is nothing wrong with it. Almost everyone passes through this phase.

But staying there creates a strange illusion. You feel you have arrived when the journey has not even opened yet.

And now you see this clearly. Your inner honesty refuses to settle for second-hand understanding.

Because deep down, you know the difference between what you’ve read and what you’ve touched within yourself.

## When Experience Replaces Explanation

Recently, someone shared their thoughts on duality and non-duality.

That is when it hit me that I rarely speak about non-duality.

Not because it lacks importance, but because I am not qualified to.

The “I” that feels is still present. The mind is still here. And as long as that remains, it would be a grave injustice to the truth itself.

I see people use this word so casually, as if understanding comes from reading or repeating it.

But some people live so weightlessly. They laugh like child and are fully in sync with life. They easily switch between heavy spiritual topics and light-hearted discussions.

And when you grow in your inner journey, you realize that reading Jiddu Krishnamurti, Nisargadatta Maharaj, or Eckhart Tolle can inspire you. But it does not mean you understand the state they speak from.

The mind understands language. It cannot grasp the space from which those words arise.

Yet, it tries.

And you know, understanding non-duality or spiritual concepts is like tasting sugar.

You can describe sugar in every possible way. Its structure, taste, and effects.

But none of it matters until it touches your tongue.

Spiritual truth works the same way.

==No explanation can replace experience.==

And once even a glimpse comes, the need to explain reduces on its own.

Because in that state, the separation between you and others dissolves. The urge to convince fades. Words lose urgency.

You can see the argument ego might love to give. “Jiddu did. Why can’t I”

But deep down, you know these masters have no compulsion to speak or share. They are/were equally calm when they sat with nothing and didn’t speak.

They are/were already in that oneness. And when they speak about it, they can easily shift their consciousness from oneness to the daily human experience of duality.

Quoting them or twisting their words to fit our narrative doesn’t actually help if it comes from compulsion.

But if the compulsion to explain comes, you know where to look and ask —

***“Is it curiosity Does the mind want to understand it further by talking, or is it trying to prove itself”***

These questions alone can reveal more than hours of reading.

## The Desert Where Words Stop Working

This realization often comes when you enter a phase in the inner journey where words no longer satisfy.

You read, listened, and reflected.

And still, there was a dryness within.

It felt like standing in a desert surrounded by descriptions of water. But not a single drop to drink.

At this point, you realized no amount of knowledge can satisfy you. Concepts that once felt profound felt distant. Unreachable.

Because the mind no longer found its usual support. The structures that gave a sense of certainty crumbled.

This phase is raw.

It strips away your outer layers, exposing the primal hunger beneath all intellectual understanding.

A deeper longing rises. Not for more information, but for the truth that can be breathed.

This is where many turn back, seeking comfort in familiar explanations.

But you stayed and allowed this dryness to exist without rushing to fill it. And moved closer to real transformation.

## The Raw Journey

You have realized that true inner change rarely comes through polished understanding.

It comes when the ground beneath your beliefs cracks. When certainty dissolves, and even your own conclusions feel incomplete.

It is deeply uncomfortable and sometimes painful.

But this breaking is not against you.

It clears space.

A space where clarity emerges. Where you stop leaning on borrowed words. Where you don’t need to prove or explain anything.

From here, the journey takes a different turn.

It becomes less about knowing and more about seeing. Less about collecting ideas and more about shedding what is not real.

And slowly, your understanding grows, without demanding compulsive expression or validation. Where you feel complete within yourself.

## A Walk Forward

Spirituality is not about how well you explain it.

It is about how deeply you have allowed it to transform you.

Words can guide you to the door. But they cannot take the step for you.

And when that step happens, even in the smallest way, more honesty and clarity arise.

You may still read, listen, and reflect.

But now, you also trust what unfolds within you.

And that trust carries you forward without noise, display, or the need to arrive anywhere.

Just a steady walk into what is real, unknown, but not scary as it might have been once.
