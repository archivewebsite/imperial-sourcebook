# Practical Grammar Notes

These notes focus on choices that change naturalness and meaning. They are not tense tables or formal syntax rules.

## Starter Notes

| Focus | Practical Point | Example | Indonesian Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Countable nouns | Use `a/an` or a plural with countable singular nouns. | `a book`, `two books` | Kata benda yang bisa dihitung perlu penanda jumlah. |
| Uncountable nouns | Do not add plural `-s` to common uncountables. | `information`, `advice`, `equipment` | Jangan tulis `informations` atau `advices`. |
| General vs specific articles | Use `the` when the listener can identify the thing. | `I saw a dog. The dog was small.` | `a` memperkenalkan; `the` menunjuk yang sudah jelas. |
| Zero article | Use no article for many general plural or uncountable ideas. | `Books are useful.` | Bukan `The books` kalau maksudnya buku secara umum. |
| Prepositions | Learn prepositions as chunks, not one-word translations. | `interested in`, `responsible for` | Preposisi Inggris sering tidak sama dengan terjemahan Indonesia. |
| Place and time | `at`, `on`, and `in` depend on the scale of time or place. | `at 7`, `on Monday`, `in April` | Hafalkan pola umum dengan contoh. |
| Word choice | Similar words often need different patterns. | `listen to music`, `hear a noise` | `listen` aktif; `hear` menerima suara. |
| `say`, `tell`, `speak`, `talk` | Use `tell` with a person object, and `say` for the words. | `She told me the truth.` | `tell me`, bukan `say me`. |
| `do` and `make` | `do` often fits tasks; `make` often fits creation or decisions. | `do homework`, `make a plan` | Banyak yang harus dihafal sebagai collocation. |
| Phrasal verbs | The particle changes the meaning of the verb. | `look up a word`, `look after a child` | Jangan terjemahkan kata per kata. |
| Modal verbs | Modals soften or change the force of a request. | `Can you help?`, `Could you help?` | `could` terdengar lebih sopan. |
| Hedging | `maybe`, `probably`, `I think`, and `kind of` soften claims. | `I think this works.` | Berguna agar tidak terdengar terlalu keras. |
| Register | Choose formal or informal words based on situation. | `request` vs `ask for` | Email resmi butuh pilihan kata lebih rapi. |
| Spoken chunks | Natural speech uses chunks, not only isolated grammar. | `That makes sense.`, `I see what you mean.` | Hafalkan sebagai frasa siap pakai. |
| Common mistake check | When unsure, check countability, preposition, word choice, and word order first. | `discuss the issue`, not `discuss about the issue` | Empat area ini sering membuat kalimat terdengar tidak alami. |
