# Cultural Notes

`Cultural Notes` stores practical German communication notes about greetings, politeness, register, and social distance.

## What Belongs Here

- language-culture notes that affect German wording
- reminders about `du/Sie`, greetings, classroom and work contexts, directness, and regional variation
- short examples with Indonesian explanations

## What Does Not

- broad tourism notes
- national stereotypes
- grammar lessons without a communication point

## How To Use It

- use these notes to choose safer wording in real situations
- keep observations practical and context-specific
- add regional differences only when they affect language choice

## Current Notes

- `Cultural Notes.md`: starter German communication notes.


## Review Rhythm

- Review when cultural examples become too broad, dated, or better handled as phrasebook material.
- Keep notes focused on communication context, not general travel or history references.
