### Usage Notes

> [!tip] Suggested hierarchy
> Use Mint for soft paper-like backgrounds, Celadon and Pistachio for muted accents, Avocado through Moss for grounded product surfaces, and Olive through Teal Green for typography, overlays, or dramatic dark panels.

> [!tip] Contrast balance
> The top three swatches still support dark text best. From Avocado downward, the photo reference shifts deep enough that light text becomes the safer default for labels, tags, and UI controls.

> [!tip] Palette character
> This photo-matched set reads quieter and more tactile than the original palette. It feels editorial, natural, and textile-led, which makes it strong for interiors, apparel, wellness, hospitality, and understated eco brands.
